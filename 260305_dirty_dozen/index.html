The 'Dirty Dozen'

Spinach

Strawberries

Spinach has more pesticide residue by weight than any other type of produce

Studies show dozens of pesticides are used on strawberries

Kale, Collard, and

Mustard Greens

Grapes

In some studies, grape samples have contained 26 different kinds of pesticides

More than half of kale samples were found to be tainted by possibly cancer-causing pesticides

Peaches

Cherries

About 59 different pesticides have been found on peaches

Cherries have been found to have residue from iprodione, a fungicide thought to be a human carcinogen

Pears

Nectarines

Pears are among the most pesticide-contaminated fruits

Studies show as many as 97 percent of nectarines have pesticide contamination

Apples

Blackberries

Apples are doused with chemicals considered to be potentially carcinogenic

Over 90 percent of samples contained pesticides residue

Blueberries

Potatoes

Blueberries contain traces of several toxic pesticides

Potatoes joined the 'Dirty Dozen' just in 2026
